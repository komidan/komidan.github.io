EasyKreedz is an open-source suite of plugins developed for Counter-Strike 1.6
with [AMX Mod X](https://www.amxmodx.org/), in which they use the
[Pawn](https://www.compuphase.com/pawn/pawn.htm) scripting language. It's goal is to make the experience more inviting for the older and newer generations of players.

A hefty goal I created for myself is for this suite to work on both the server
and LAN side. This can be difficult depending on certain plugin implementations
and what-not, and I've yet to decide how to handle that! Soon™.

## What is "Kreedz"?

Kreedz named after a very old player who initially brought to light the concept
of using Valve's source engine physics to air-strafe and bhop, traversing
difficult obstacles. It has developed into something similar to speedrunning
where people record their runs, and document their times on community driven
forums. People can then compete for the best times.

## Motivations

The KZ-Rush Lan Pack is closed-source, For a very old community built by
predecesors from the early 2000s, it's unfortunately slowly dying. In an attempt
to possibly revive it, or just create a good tool for the hardcore fans, I
wanted an open-source solution. This way, aspiring developers can get their feet
wet and contribute to this project over some big open-source project, and it's
something they can be passionate about.
